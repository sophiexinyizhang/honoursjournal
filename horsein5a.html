Proof of concept/quick test

PRECEDENTS & CONTEXT

Always been a little project idea I've had, I've seen some really cool reels of graffiti sticker art frame-by-frame animation.

PROCESS & METHODS

  • Edit & export individual frames of an animation (in this case I used Muybridge's Horse in Motion) in Photoshop
  • Print onto A4 sticker paper
  • Cut out frames (don't get the order mixed up!!!)
  • Stick wherever you like!

REFLECTION ON ACTION

  • Love Muybridge's Horse in Motion (which I studied in 2nd year) & also tying it to a lot of cool design work I've seen this year especially around CNY for 2026 Year of the Horse
  • Would be nicer if it was higher-res

REFLECTION FOR ACTION
