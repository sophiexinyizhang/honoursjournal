SOPHIE ZHANG A1 OVERVIEW MAP A2 ANNOTATED PROCESS

In A Crit,

in a crit, my tutor suggested i look at more interesting places of exploring through my practice. more interesting than the mundane staircase of an incredibly corporate building uts, or my very white hallway of a very white house that leads to my bedroom, these places of mundanity and everydayness. he said the word mundane in a careful tread no offence intended. but no offence taken really as it is precisely this everyday mundanity that i love so dearly.

i couldn't care less for places like the landmarks of sydney, that opera house on that glistening harbour. i don't care about these places of prestige, of objective beauty, of default 'interesting-ness'.

i care about more liminal, daily places like that car tunnel, that infinite place from perks of being a wallflower, like a teenager standing out from the sunroof, watching the fluorescent orange lights race past to a david bowie song.

that infinitely present moment, made interesting not by default but by our perception of living it.
