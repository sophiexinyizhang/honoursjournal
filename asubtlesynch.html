A Subtle Synchronicity

@3ditional

Yama11 [@3ditional] (2025)

I thought it apt to begin my bibliography where my research began this year: scrolling on my TikTok feed. I chose this frame-by-frame graffiti animation by Yama11 (@3ditional) from a plethora of graffiti stopmotion videos out there because of the caption, where Yama11 notes a "subtle synchronicity" between shifting light and shadow and the rotation of the painted letters. Uncontrolled details like the rhythm of passing traffic in the background and flicker of changing light unexpectedly align with the rhythm of the music.

This accidental harmony turns a meticulously planned animation into something unexpectedly poetic–a "reorder[ing of] the elements of chaos into a harmonic form." Remediated and disseminated as a TikTok video, graffiti is not just a static mark, but a temporal experience shaped by its surroundings. Situated beneath a bridge (interestingly in Zurich 'the pearl of Europe'), the work centres an anonymous underpass, redirecting attention away from Eurocentric places of prestige and toward the overlooked everyday.

Reframing ordinary urban spaces through observation and play consequently became the gateway point of my project. How can something as subjective as the light and shadow under a bridge become a fleeting intervention for a wider audience? How can designing in small places and ways draw attention to the minutiae of the anonymous everyday?
