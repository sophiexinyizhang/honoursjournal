Are Membership Models The Future of Independent Media?

AIGA Eye on Design

Words by Zachary Petit
Published on September 15th, 2022

"The key to sustaining independent creative enterprises in a capitalistic landscape may just be to share the keys with readers."

EVALUATION OF SOURCE

This piece is written by Zachary Petit for AIGA Eye on Design, published in 2022. Eye on Design itself is a multimedia editorial platform, with a print magazine and newsletter. AIGA itself has a membership model offering connection to a large design community in the US and several other member benefits. The article references the COVID-19 pandemic's impact on the media industry and the consequent need for contemporary independent media to evolve.

SUMMARY

Petit proposes a possible, if idealistic, vision for the future of independent media to lie in membership models using several examples of organisations like Real Review, gal-dem and Riposte. By exploring the evolution of contemporary digital publications and how the funding of their business models change, Petit makes a case where membership models mainly in the form of tiered membership plans exemplify a symbiotic relationship between publisher and reader. By moving away from the digital advertising, buzz words and clicks of traditional online marketing and attention economy, this membership model becomes "a way for our communities to connect with what we do and to directly empower us to do it", ensuring to maintain the accessibility of its services.

INTERPRETATION

I especially love the part of the article where Riposte founder and editor Danielle Pender said "if we only publish things that get a lot of eyeballs and clicks, aka make money, what is happening to the stories and people that don't drive traffic? Does that mean their stories aren't as valid or worthwhile?" As someone who's an unhappy participant of the attention economy and monetised ads, sponsorships and endless screen time, I love this alternative business model for independent media to survive under a community of those who give what they can, and what this can do for a world where voices and opinions of anything (even and especially those I disagree with) can be expressed. Facilitating open conversation, dialogue and free democratic journalism are all extremely important values of mine.

"That's why independent media is more important than ever. We need different voices and different stories and a wider perspective on all sorts of issues — even if we don't totally agree."
